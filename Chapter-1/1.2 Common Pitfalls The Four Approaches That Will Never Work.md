## 1.2 Common Pitfalls: The Four Approaches That Will Never Work

Before applying the diagnostic lens, confirm you are not defaulting to one of these structurally flawed approaches. Each one treats voice bleed as a single undifferentiated problem rather than five separate, addressable failures.

| Approach | What You Do | Why It Fails Structurally | What It Actually Addresses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vague Re-Prompting** | Type "make him sound grumpier" and regenerate | Emotional adjectives shift tone only; they say nothing about cadence, vocabulary, sentence variance, or conversational rhythm | ~10% of Emotional Register only |
| **Manual Rewriting** | Generate, then rewrite every line by hand | Defeats the purpose of AI as a time-saver; produces no reusable correction | All five dimensions, but at the cost of hours per scene |
| **Style-Mimicry Pasting** | Paste a favorite author's paragraph into the prompt | Mimics surface rhythm without the psychological architecture beneath it | Cadence partially; no vocabulary, variance, register, or rhythm |
| **AI Abandonment** | Write all dialogue by hand | Surrenders the speed advantage; unsustainable at rapid-release pace | All five dimensions manually; zero scalability |

**Self-Check:** If you catch yourself reaching for any of these four approaches, pause. Run the five-dimensional scan first. Name the failing channels. Then correct with precision.
