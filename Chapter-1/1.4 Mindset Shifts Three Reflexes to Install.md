## 1.4 Mindset Shifts: Three Reflexes to Install

Read each shift aloud. Write it on a sticky note. Place it beside your writing screen. These three reflexes replace the old delete-and-retry loop.

**Shift 1 — Diagnose Before You Feel**
When output sounds wrong, pause for three seconds. Run the five-dimensional scan. Name the failing channels. Then reach for the correction. Vitals first. Intervention second.

**Shift 2 — Voice Is Measurable Architecture**
Voice is built from specifiable components: rhythm, word choice, sentence variation, emotional temperature, conversational movement. The AI has parameters you have not yet specified with precision. You are handing the AI exact specifications, not hoping it gets lucky.

**Shift 3 — Thirty Seconds Now Saves Two Hours Later**
The diagnostic step adds roughly thirty seconds. Re-prompting without diagnosis is guessing. Guard those thirty seconds. They pay you back a hundredfold in every draft that follows.
