## 1.1 Core Framework: The Five-Dimensional Diagnostic Lens

Use this framework every time you read AI-generated dialogue that sounds flat. Scan each dimension independently. Name what collapsed. Then correct only the failing channels.

| # | Dimension | What It Governs | Diagnostic Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cadence** | Rhythm and pacing of speech | Are the sentences delivered at a uniform tempo, or do they alternate between clipped bursts and longer controlled lines? |
| 2 | **Vocabulary Range** | Ceiling and floor of word choices | Are the words polite and mid-register, or do they match the character's specific texture (blunt, lyrical, technical, colloquial)? |
| 3 | **Sentence-Length Variance** | Fluctuation in sentence length within a single speech | Are all sentences roughly the same length (metronomic), or do they alternate dynamically? |
| 4 | **Emotional Register** | Baseline emotional temperature and expressiveness | Is the tone uniformly warm and courteous, or does it match the character's specific temperature (guarded, effusive, sardonic, tender)? |
| 5 | **Conversational Rhythm** | How the character enters, sustains, and exits exchanges | Does the character wait politely and respond fully, or do they interrupt, deflect, trail off, or barrel through? |

**Quick-Reference Rule:** Voice bleed occurs when one or more dimensions collapses toward the AI's default register: medium-length sentences, polite mid-range vocabulary, uniform emotional warmth, steady predictable rhythm, and orderly turn-taking. If your character sounds like a well-meaning colleague in a professional meeting, all five dials have been snapped to neutral.
