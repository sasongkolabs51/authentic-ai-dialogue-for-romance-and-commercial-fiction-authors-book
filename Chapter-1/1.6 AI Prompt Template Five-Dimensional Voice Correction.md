## 1.6 AI Prompt Template: Five-Dimensional Voice Correction

Use this template to translate your Voice Bleed Audit findings into a structured corrective prompt. Replace every bracketed field with the specific findings from your audit and benchmark. The more precise your language, the more accurately the AI will reproduce the target voice.

---

**Copy the prompt below into your AI tool. Fill in every bracket before generating.**

> Write dialogue for [CHARACTER_NAME] in the following scene: [BRIEF_SCENE_DESCRIPTION_IN_1_TO_2_SENTENCES].
>
> Apply these five linguistic constraints precisely:
>
> **Cadence:** [DESCRIBE_TARGET_RHYTHM — e.g., "Clipped fragments. Abrupt stops. Cuts off the other speaker midsentence. Never finishes a thought he started."]
>
> **Vocabulary Range:** [DESCRIBE_TARGET_WORD_CHOICES — e.g., "Monosyllabic. Blunt. Hard consonants. No words longer than two syllables unless absolutely necessary. No softening language."]
>
> **Sentence-Length Variance:** [DESCRIBE_TARGET_VARIANCE_PATTERN — e.g., "Alternate between 2–4 word bursts and one longer controlled sentence (15–20 words) used to rein himself in. No two consecutive sentences should be the same length."]
>
> **Emotional Register:** [DESCRIBE_TARGET_TEMPERATURE — e.g., "Cold. Controlled. Irritation leaking through the cracks. Never warm. Never forgiving. If he says 'it's fine,' the reader should feel the temperature drop."]
>
> **Conversational Rhythm:** [DESCRIBE_TARGET_TURN_TAKING — e.g., "Interrupts. Deflects questions with counter-questions. Shuts down the exchange before the other character finishes apologizing. Physically turns away midconversation."]
>
> Do NOT use polite transitional phrases. Do NOT use collaborative language. Do NOT resolve emotional tension within the exchange. Leave the scene emotionally unresolved.

---

**Post-Generation Check:** Compare the output to your Voice Benchmark from Exercise 2. If one dimension still feels slightly off, adjust only that dimension's instruction and regenerate.
