# Chapter 1: Diagnose the Voice Bleed — A Five-Dimensional Diagnostic Workbook
*A practical workbook for applying the concepts from the chapter.*

## How to Use These Materials

This workbook converts the diagnostic framework from Chapter 1 into immediately usable tools. You will find a reference framework, a troubleshooting guide, a worked case study, structured worksheets, an AI prompt template, and a reflection exercise. Work through the sections in order during a single thirty-minute session. You will need one AI-generated dialogue sample from your current work-in-progress, a blank document or notebook, and a timer. By the end, you will have a completed diagnostic map showing exactly which linguistic dimensions your AI tool is flattening and a corrected prompt ready to generate differentiated voice on the next pass.
