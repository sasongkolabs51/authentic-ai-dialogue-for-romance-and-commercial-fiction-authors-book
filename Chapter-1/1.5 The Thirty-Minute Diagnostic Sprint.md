## 1.5 The Thirty-Minute Diagnostic Sprint

Complete all three exercises below in a single session. Total time: thirty minutes. You will produce three artifacts: a Voice Bleed Audit, a Voice Benchmark, and a Failed-Approach Autopsy.

### 1.5.1 Setup Checklist

Confirm you have everything before starting the timer.

- [ ] One AI-generated dialogue sample from your current WIP (a scene with at least two speaking characters)
- [ ] A pen and notebook, or a blank document open on screen
- [ ] A timer set for thirty minutes
- [ ] Permission to be honest about what you see, without flinching or self-blame
