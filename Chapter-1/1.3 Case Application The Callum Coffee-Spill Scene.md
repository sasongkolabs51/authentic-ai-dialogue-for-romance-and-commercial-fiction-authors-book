## 1.3 Case Application: The Callum Coffee-Spill Scene

Use this worked example as a calibration reference when running your own audits. It demonstrates how a single flat output fails across all five dimensions simultaneously.

**Scene:** Grumpy hero Callum is working at a café. Sunshine heroine Mara spills coffee on his manuscript. The prompt specified "grumpy and guarded."

**Flat AI Output:**
> *"Oh, that's quite all right. I appreciate you being honest about it. Perhaps we can find a way to clean this up together. I understand these things happen."*

**Five-Dimensional Breakdown:**

| Dimension | What the AI Produced | What Callum Should Sound Like |
|---|---|---|
| **Cadence** | Uniform rhythm; four sentences at the same unhurried pace | Clipped fragments; abrupt stops; cuts Mara off mid-apology |
| **Vocabulary Range** | Polite, mid-register, collaborative ("I appreciate," "Perhaps," "Together") | Short, blunt monosyllables; hard consonants; language that closes doors |
| **Sentence-Length Variance** | All sentences 12–15 words; metronomic | Alternates between three-word snaps ("Great. Just great.") and one longer controlled sentence |
| **Emotional Register** | Warm, forgiving, collaborative | Cold, controlled; irritation leaking through cracks like steam from a sealed lid |
| **Conversational Rhythm** | Waits for Mara to finish; responds fully; invites collaboration | Interrupts; deflects; shuts down the exchange; turns away while she is still mid-sentence |

**Key Insight:** Typing "make him sound grumpier" addresses roughly 10% of the Emotional Register dimension and ignores the other four entirely. All five channels require independent correction.
