## 2.5 The Ninety-Second Voice Verification Read

Perform this scan after every generation, before committing output to your manuscript. Set a timer for ninety seconds. Read the dialogue five quick passes—one per dimension. The speed prevents overthinking and spiraling.

**Checklist: Ninety-Second Voice Verification Read**

- [ ] **Cadence** – Does the rhythm match my target pattern? (Fragments where I specified fragments. Longer sentences only where I allowed them.)
- [ ] **Vocabulary Range** – Are the words within my ceiling and floor? (No unexpected jargon. No unexpected simplicity.)
- [ ] **Sentence-Length Variance** – Do the lengths fluctuate within my Variance Band? (No metronomic uniformity.)
- [ ] **Emotional Register** – Does the temperature match my Baseline setting? (Guarded stays guarded. Volatile stays volatile.) Is the Leakage Rate correct?
- [ ] **Conversational Rhythm** – Does the character enter, sustain, and exit the exchange according to my Turn-Taking Style and Exit Pattern?

**Branching Result:**

- **All five boxes checked →** You can commit the output to your manuscript and move forward with confidence.
- **One box unchecked →** Adjust that single modifier, regenerate the output, and verify it again.
- **Two or more boxes unchecked →** Escalate the issue and follow the Decision Tree in section 2.6.
