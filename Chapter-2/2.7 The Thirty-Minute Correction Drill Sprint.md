## 2.7 The Thirty-Minute Correction Drill Sprint

Complete all three drills below in a single session. Total time: thirty minutes. You will produce a Three-Prompt Rewrite Log, a Verification Drill Log, and a Cross-Platform Stability Test. Together, these three artifacts are your personal evidence that the methodology works on your characters, in your genre, under your deadline.

### 2.7.1 Setup Checklist

Confirm you have everything before starting the timer.

- [ ] Chapter 1 diagnostic worksheet open beside you (the one with Mild / Moderate / Severe ratings)
- [ ] Three prompts from your current WIP where voice bleed occurred
- [ ] Your AI tool open and ready
- [ ] A blank document or notebook for logging results
- [ ] Timer set for thirty minutes
