## 2.4 AI Prompt Template: Stacked Modifier Template

Use this template every time you generate dialogue for a character whose voice keeps flattening. Replace every bracketed field with the specific findings from your Chapter 1 Voice Bleed Audit. Stack two or three modifiers targeting the dimensions that scored "Moderate" or "Severe" in your audit.

---

**Copy the prompt below into your AI tool. Fill in every bracket before generating.**

> Write dialogue for [CHARACTER_NAME] in the following scene: [BRIEF_SCENE_DESCRIPTION_IN_1_TO_2_SENTENCES].
>
> Apply these linguistic constraints simultaneously:
>
> **Cadence – [MODIFIER_NAME, e.g., Rhythm Lock]:** [SPECIFIC_CONSTRAINT — e.g., "Alternate between 2–4 word fragments and one controlled sentence of 12–15 words. Never produce two consecutive sentences of the same length."]
>
> **Vocabulary Range – [MODIFIER_NAME, e.g., Ceiling Cap]:** [SPECIFIC_CONSTRAINT — e.g., "Restrict all word choices to monosyllabic or two-syllable words. No abstract nouns. No softening qualifiers."]
>
> **Emotional Register – [MODIFIER_NAME, e.g., Baseline Temperature]:** [SPECIFIC_CONSTRAINT — e.g., "Cold and controlled. Irritation surfaces only through clipped syntax and physical action beats. No warm or collaborative language."]
>
> Do NOT use polite transitional phrases. Do NOT resolve emotional tension within the exchange.

---

**Post-Generation Rule:** Right after you finish, do the Ninety-Second Voice Verification Read from section 2.5. If one part of the voice feels a little off, change only that one constraint line and generate again. All the other settings in your stack stay the same.
