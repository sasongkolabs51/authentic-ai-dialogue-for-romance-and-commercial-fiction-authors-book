# Chapter 2: Precision Prompt Corrections — The Twelve-Modifier Workbook
*A practical workbook for applying the concepts from the chapter.*

## How to Use These Materials

This workbook converts the correction methodology from Chapter 2 into immediately usable tools. You will find a modifier reference framework, a worked case study, an AI prompt template, a verification checklist, an escalation decision tree, and a three-drill practice sprint. Work through sections 2.1 through 2.6 as reference material first, then complete the drills in section 2.7 during a single thirty-minute session. You will need your completed Chapter 1 Voice Bleed Audit, your AI tool, three prompts from your current work-in-progress where voice bleed occurred, and a timer. By the end, you will have rewritten three prompts using precision modifiers, verified the corrections in ninety seconds each, and documented measurable improvement across all five dimensions.
