## 7.1 Common Pitfalls: Why Scattered Voice Notes Collapse at Scale

Before building the library, confirm you are not relying on one of these three structurally broken approaches. Each one fails for a specific reason that the library is designed to solve.

| Approach | What You Do | Why It Fails at Scale | The Consequence by Book Five |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Copy-pasting old prompts** | Pull a prompt from a previous book and reuse it | Matrices and filters evolve; old versions contradict refined ones | Voice drifts invisibly; you cannot identify which version introduced the shift |
| **Single master prompt** | Paste all characters' matrices into one system prompt | The AI averages multiple character instructions into a bland composite | Every character sounds like a polite, mildly differentiated version of the same person |
| **Scattered documents and memory** | Keep voice notes across multiple files, sticky notes, and Notion pages | Retrieval time grows with document count; memory degrades under deadline pressure | You settle for the wrong version because you cannot find the right one in time |

**The Fix:** Build a single, centralized, version-controlled library with a consistent naming convention and folder structure. The library replaces searching with navigating, guessing with retrieving, and dread with sixty-second deployment.
