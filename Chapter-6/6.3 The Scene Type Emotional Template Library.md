## 6.3 The Scene Type Emotional Template Library

### 6.3.1 Scene Type Selection Checklist

Ask yourself what the scene is doing in the story. Check the one that matches.

- [ ] Two characters meeting for the first time, attraction sparking through awkwardness → **Meet-Cute**
- [ ] The first physical moment of connection, anticipation at its peak → **First Kiss**
- [ ] Characters talking past each other, each saying one thing and meaning another → **Misunderstanding**
- [ ] One character dropping all defenses to declare something aloud for the first time → **Grand Gesture**
- [ ] The core wound cracking open, the cruelest or most final words spoken → **Dark Moment**
- [ ] Two characters cautiously approaching each other after a rupture → **Reconciliation**
- [ ] Affection expressed through teasing, callbacks, and playful one-upmanship → **Banter-Heavy Montage**
- [ ] One character handing another person something emotionally fragile → **Intimate Vulnerability**

### 6.3.2 The Eight Scene Type Templates

**The Meet-Cute**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | Light, curious, charged with first-impression energy. |
| **Subtext Density** | Low. Attraction is expressed through observation and reaction rather than heavy unspoken weight. |
| **Pacing** | Quick exchanges, interruptions, overlapping speech. The rhythm of two people talking past and around each other before connecting. |

**The First Kiss**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | Moderate to high. Anticipation builds through the scene. |
| **Subtext Density** | Medium to heavy. The characters circle the moment, saying everything except the thing. |
| **Pacing** | Slow acceleration. Exchanges lengthen as the scene approaches the kiss, then a sharp compression at the moment of contact. |

**The Misunderstanding**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | Moderate to high. Frustration and hurt compress the dialogue. |
| **Subtext Density** | Heavy. Each character says one thing and means another. The reader sees both layers. |
| **Pacing** | Uneven. One character pushes. The other deflects. The rhythm breaks with interruptions and subject changes. |

**The Grand Gesture**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | High. Vulnerability breaks through the character's default register. |
| **Subtext Density** | Low to medium. The emotional truth is spoken aloud for the first time. |
| **Pacing** | Building to a single sustained monologue or declaration, then a pause, then the response. |

**The Dark Moment**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | Peak. The character's core wound is fully activated. |
| **Subtext Density** | Heavy. The character says the cruelest or most final thing they can manage. The subtext carries the grief underneath. |
| **Pacing** | Compressed. Short exchanges, silences, one devastating line, then withdrawal. |

**The Reconciliation**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | Moderate. Exhaustion and tentative hope modulate the register. |
| **Subtext Density** | Medium. The characters test whether the other person is still safe to approach. |
| **Pacing** | Slow, halting. False starts and restarted sentences. The rhythm of two people rebuilding trust in real time. |

**The Banter-Heavy Montage**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | Light to moderate. Affection is expressed through teasing and one-upmanship. |
| **Subtext Density** | Light. The subtext is "I enjoy you" rather than heavy emotional weight. |
| **Pacing** | Rapid-fire. Overlapping dialogue, callbacks, interruptions. The rhythm of two people who are genuinely having fun. |

**The Intimate Vulnerability**

| Parameter | Configuration |
|---|---|
| **Intensity** | High. The character's defenses are fully down. |
| **Subtext Density** | Medium. The character says the true thing but still manages it, still shapes how it lands. |
| **Pacing** | Slow. Long pauses, trailing sentences, one word at a time. The rhythm of someone handing another person something fragile. |
