### 6.7.2 Thirty-Five-Minute Pacing Guide

| Time Block | Step | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–5:00 | Step 1 | Identify subgenre. Apply calibration parameters. |
| 5:00–12:00 | Step 2 | Select three scene types. Apply templates. Stack layers. |

### 6.7.3 Save and Archive Protocol

- [ ] File name follows this format: **[CHARACTER_NAME] — Calibrated Emotional Filter — [SUBGENRE] — v1.0 — [DATE]**
- [ ] File is stored alongside the Chapter 5 Emotional Filters in your project directory
- [ ] You can locate and open the file in under ten seconds
- [ ] You have read the calibrated filter aloud one final time and felt the genre's emotional texture activate
- [ ] You have resisted the urge to make one more adjustment after the comparison confirmed all three scenes hold

**Troubleshooting note:** If one scene type consistently goes too far or not far enough after two adjustment passes, check how you applied your layers. You might have placed the scene-type template on top of the genre calibration, which is the correct approach. You might have replaced the genre calibration with the template instead, which causes the problem. These two layers stack together. The genre calibration sets the overall intensity, density, and pacing range for your story. The scene-type template then modulates the scene within that range. If you find that you replaced the calibration rather than stacking the template on top, reapply both layers and regenerate.
