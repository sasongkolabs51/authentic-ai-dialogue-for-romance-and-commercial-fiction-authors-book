## 6.2 The Genre Calibration Reference Chart

### 6.2.1 The Five Subgenre Profiles

Locate your subgenre below. The three calibration parameters—Emotional Intensity Range, Subtext Density Level, and Pacing Curve—govern how your Three-Tier Emotional Filter delivers its content for your specific readership.

**Contemporary Romance**

| Parameter | Calibration |
|---|---|
| **Emotional Intensity Range** | Moderate, warm, with controlled spikes during conflict scenes. |
| **Subtext Density Level** | Medium. The subtext becomes visible to the reader within two to three exchanges. |
| **Pacing Curve** | Steady. Emotional beats land every four to six lines. A release or shift occurs within the scene. |

**Dark Romance**

| Parameter | Calibration |
|---|---|
| **Emotional Intensity Range** | High to volatile. Sustained tension with sharp spikes during confrontation. |
| **Subtext Density Level** | Heavy. The subtext remains partially hidden for five to eight exchanges. The reader senses the weight without seeing it clearly. |
| **Pacing Curve** | Slow burn with sudden acceleration. Emotional beats build over eight to twelve lines before a sharp turn or crack. |

**Rom-Com**

| Parameter | Calibration |
|---|---|
| **Emotional Intensity Range** | Light to moderate. Comedic deflection manages the emotional charge. |
| **Subtext Density Level** | Light to medium. The subtext is visible and playful rather than heavy or punishing. |
| **Pacing Curve** | Rapid-fire. Emotional beats land every two to three lines. Deflections and recoveries cycle quickly. The emotional truth surfaces in brief, sharp moments between the humor. |

**Romantic Suspense**

| Parameter | Calibration |
|---|---|
| **Emotional Intensity Range** | Moderate to high. External danger modulates the emotional register. |
| **Subtext Density Level** | Medium to heavy. Trust and suspicion layer every exchange. |
| **Pacing Curve** | Uneven. Emotional beats are interrupted by plot pressure, creating a push-pull rhythm that keeps the reader off-balance. |

**Historical Romance**

| Parameter | Calibration |
|---|---|
| **Emotional Intensity Range** | Moderate. Formality and social constraint compress the emotional expression. |
| **Subtext Density Level** | Heavy. Propriety and restraint force the subtext to carry most of the emotional weight. |
| **Pacing Curve** | Slow burn. Emotional beats build over six to ten lines. The release is delayed by social convention. The tension is sustained by what cannot be said aloud. |
