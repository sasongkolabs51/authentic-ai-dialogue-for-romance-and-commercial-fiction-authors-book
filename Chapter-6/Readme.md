# Chapter 6: Calibrate Emotion to Genre and Scene — The Subgenre Tuning and Scene-Type Template Workbook
*A practical workbook for applying the concepts from the chapter.*

## How to Use These Materials

This workbook converts the genre-calibration and scene-type tuning system from Chapter 6 into a complete, ready-to-use calibration toolkit. You will find a subgenre identification self-assessment, a five-subgenre reference chart, eight scene-type emotional templates, a three-layer stacking system, a pregeneration verification checklist, a three-scene verification workflow with decision tree, and a thirty-five-minute sprint plan. Work through sections 6.1 through 6.5 to establish your calibration settings, then complete the verification workflow in section 6.6 and the sprint in section 6.7. You will need your Chapter 5 Three-Tier Emotional Filters, your AI tool, and a timer. By the end, your emotional filters will be tuned to the precise frequency your subgenre's readership expects and rewards, verified across three scene types, and documented for every future generation pass.
