## 6.6 The Three-Scene Verification Workflow

### 6.6.1 Action Plan

1. **Select** three types of scenes that show different emotional tones: one with high tension, one that feels tender, and one that is light.
2. **Generate** six to eight lines of dialogue for each scene using your adjusted filter.
3. **Check** each set of dialogue against the five-question Reader Expectation Alignment checklist.
4. **Adjust** any failing setting by one step, then regenerate and check again.
5. **Confirm** that all three scenes fit within the target range for your genre.
6. **Lock** your settings and move forward.

**Recommended testing combination:**
- **High-Tension:** Dark Moment or Misunderstanding
- **Tender:** First Kiss or Intimate Vulnerability
- **Light:** Meet-Cute or Banter-Heavy Montage

### 6.6.3 Decision Tree: Diagnosing a Calibration Miss

**Step 1 – Which scene drifted?**

- High-Tension → Proceed to Step 2a.
- Tender → Proceed to Step 2b.
- Light → Proceed to Step 2c.

**Step 2a – High-Tension scene drift:**

| Symptom | Failing Parameter | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Too flat, not enough edge | Intensity Range | One increment upward |
| Subtext is too visible, too quickly | Subtext Density | One increment heavier |
| The scene resolves too quickly | Pacing Curve | Add a slower build clause |

**Step 2b – Tender scene drift:**

| Symptom | Failing Parameter | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Too intense, reads as melodrama | Intensity Range | One increment downward |
| The vulnerability feels stated rather than shaped | Subtext Density | Add a management clause |
| The pacing rushes the moment | Pacing Curve | Add a deceleration instruction |

**Step 2c – Light scene drift:**

| Symptom | Failing Parameter | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Too heavy, reads as forced | Intensity Range | One increment downward |
| The humor carries hidden weight it shouldn't | Subtext Density | One increment lighter |
| The exchanges drag | Pacing Curve | Add a rapid-fire modifier |

Make one adjustment at a time, and regenerate only the scene where the voice has drifted. Run the Reader Expectation Alignment checklist again, then confirm the fix. Once it checks out, move on to the next drift if one exists.

### 6.6.4 Verification Completion Standard

All boxes checked means your calibration is done.

- [ ] All three scenes generated successfully using the calibrated filter.
- [ ] All three scenes passed the five-question Reader Expectation Alignment checklist.
- [ ] The high-tension scene feels distinctly heavier than the tender scene.
- [ ] The tender scene feels distinctly more vulnerable than the light scene.
- [ ] The light scene feels distinctly more playful than the other two.
- [ ] All three scenes exist within the same genre-specific emotional contract.
- [ ] I have completed no more than two adjustment passes.

If all boxes check, lock the settings and move forward with your draft. Additional tweaking beyond two adjustment passes produces diminishing returns. You can refine further as you generate more scenes throughout your manuscript.
