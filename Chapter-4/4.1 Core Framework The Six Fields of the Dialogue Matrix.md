## 4.1 Core Framework: The Six Fields of the Dialogue Matrix

The Dialogue Matrix is a structured document with six mandatory fields. Each field translates psychological data from your Chapter 3 profile into a concrete linguistic constraint the AI can execute. Use this reference to understand what each field governs before you begin populating it.

| Field | What It Governs | What It Prevents |
|---|---|---|
| **Cadence Signature** | Rhythm and pacing of speech; the ratio of short bursts to longer controlled sentences | Metronomic uniformity where every sentence lands at the same tempo |
| **Vocabulary Ceiling** | Upper and lower bounds of word choices; maximum complexity and minimum register | Drift into the AI's polite mid-range lexicon where every character sounds the same |
| **Sentence Architecture** | Length variance, fragment-to-completion ratio, tendency toward questions vs. declarations vs. trailing | Twelve-word monotony where every sentence stretches to identical length |
| **Emotional Default** | Baseline emotional temperature and expressiveness level; the rate at which feeling leaks through | Default warm, courteous neutrality where everyone sounds mildly delighted |
| **Conversational Rhythm** | How the character enters, sustains, and exits exchanges; interruption patterns and exit strategies | Orderly, wait-your-turn dialogue where every character lets the other finish |
| **Signature Tics** | Small, idiosyncratic verbal behaviors that make the voice feel inhabited | Generic, performed dialogue that could belong to any character |
