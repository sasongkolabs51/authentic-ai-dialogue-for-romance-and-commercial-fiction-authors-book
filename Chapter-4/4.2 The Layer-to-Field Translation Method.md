### 4.2.3 Constraint Statement Quality Check

Run each constraint statement through this checklist before finalizing your matrix. Thirty seconds per field.

- [ ] The statement uses imperative, parameter-level language (e.g., "Alternate between…" / "Never exceed…" / "Reduce to…")
- [ ] The statement contains a measurable boundary (a number, a range, a specific word, a named behavior)
- [ ] The statement describes what the language does, not what the character feels
- [ ] You could hand this statement to a stranger and they could produce dialogue matching it without reading your character's backstory
- [ ] The statement is distinct from the other fields (no redundancy across Cadence, Vocabulary, and Sentence Architecture)

Whenever a statement falls short, make it sharper by adding a specific number, naming the exact word, and defining the clear boundary.
