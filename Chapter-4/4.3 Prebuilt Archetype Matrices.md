## 4.3 Prebuilt Archetype Matrices

### 4.3.1 Archetype Identification Self-Assessment

Answer the three questions below to identify which archetype template sits closest to your character. Use it as a starting scaffold, then adapt with your own Chapter 3 data.

**1. When your character is hurt, do they:**
- [ ] Go quiet, monosyllabic, and physically withdraw → **Grumpy Hero** lean
- [ ] Talk more, overexplain, fill the silence with words → **Sunshine Heroine** lean
- [ ] Maintain composure, deflect with wit, reveal nothing → **Morally Grey** lean

**2. When your character wants something, do they:**
- [ ] Minimize the ask, make it sound like no effort, apologize for needing it → **Grumpy Hero** lean
- [ ] Ask warmly but check three times whether it's okay to ask → **Sunshine Heroine** lean
- [ ] State the want as a fact, without asking permission → **Morally Grey** lean

**3. When your character is in a room full of people, their speech:**
- [ ] Shortens. They exit conversations early. They deflect with directives → **Grumpy Hero** lean
- [ ] Lengthens. They ask questions. They make everyone feel included → **Sunshine Heroine** lean
- [ ] Becomes measured. They wait. They respond with one precise line → **Morally Grey** lean

Whichever column you checked most often is your starting template.

### 4.3.2 The Three Archetype Templates

**The Grumpy Hero**

| Field | Constraint Statements |
|---|---|
| **Cadence Signature** | Fragments of two to four words alternating with one controlled sentence of twelve to fifteen words. |
| **Vocabulary Ceiling** | Monosyllabic, blunt, Anglo-Saxon roots. No intensifiers. |
| **Sentence Architecture** | High variance. Fragments to controlled statements. Trails off under vulnerability. |
| **Emotional Default** | Cold, controlled. One microleak of vulnerability per exchange, immediately suppressed. |
| **Conversational Rhythm** | Interrupts. Deflects. Exits via directive or subject change. |
| **Signature Tics** | Contractions only. Never says "I feel." Ends uncertain statements as questions. Uses "fine" as a conversation-terminator. |

**The Sunshine Heroine**

| Field | Constraint Statements |
|---|---|
| **Cadence Signature** | Accelerating run-on sentences that build momentum. Three questions before one answer. |
| **Vocabulary Ceiling** | Effusive, intensifier-heavy, colloquial. Reaches for metaphor. |
| **Sentence Architecture** | Long compound sentences that stack clauses. High variance. Rarely fragments. |
| **Emotional Default** | Warm, open, high expressiveness. Anxiety leaking through overexplanation. |
| **Conversational Rhythm** | Fills silence. Asks before answering. Agrees verbally while deflecting emotionally. |
| **Signature Tics** | Uses "okay so" as a reset phrase. Stacks qualifiers ("really, truly, genuinely"). Trails into laughter when uncomfortable. |

**The Morally Grey Love Interest**

| Field | Constraint Statements |
|---|---|
| **Cadence Signature** | Measured, deliberate pacing. Each sentence fully formed before the next begins. |
| **Vocabulary Ceiling** | Precise, slightly formal. No slang. Occasional cutting wit. |
| **Sentence Architecture** | Uniform length. Controlled. No fragments. No trailing. |
| **Emotional Default** | Cool amusement masking calculation. One flash of genuine feeling per chapter. |
| **Conversational Rhythm** | Waits. Lets the other speaker fill silence. Responds with a single precise line. |
| **Signature Tics** | Uses the other character's name in address. Never asks questions (makes statements that function as questions). Uses "interesting" as a deflection. |
