## 4.5 The Refinement Loop

### 4.5.1 The Refinement Loop Protocol

Most matrices require two to three iteration cycles before the voice stabilizes. Follow this sequence for each cycle.

**Cycle [1 / 2 / 3]:**

1. **Generate.** Paste your current matrix into the AI tool. Generate one stress-test scene (whichever drifted last).
2. **Verify.** Run the Ninety-Second Voice Verification Read. Note which dimensions hold and which drift.
3. **Identify.** Name the single field most responsible for the drift. Write it down.
4. **Adjust.** Change that one field. Add one constraint, tighten one boundary, or replace one vague phrase with a specific parameter.
5. **Regenerate.** Run the same scene again with the updated matrix.
6. **Confirm.** Reverify. Did the drift resolve? If yes, move to the next drifting field. If no, sharpen the constraint further and regenerate once more.
7. **Log.** Record the result in the Iteration Tracker below.

**Key discipline:** Adjust one field at a time. If you change three fields simultaneously and the output shifts, you cannot identify which change caused the improvement.

### 4.5.3 Decision Tree: Am I Done Iterating?

**Question 1:** Have you completed at least two full rounds of iteration?

- **No →** Keep iterating, because you are still in the normal range for this process.
- **Yes →** Move on to Question 2.

**Question 2:** Does the voice stay consistent across all three stress-test scenes: conflict, flirtation, and vulnerability?

- **No →** Find the scene where the voice drifts. Adjust the field responsible for that drift. Regenerate the output. Verify the voice again.
- **Yes →** Move on to Question 3.

**Question 3:** Have you already completed three or more rounds?

- **No →** Your matrix is finished. Save it, label it, and move forward.
- **Yes →** Stop here. Your matrix is finished. More tweaking will give you smaller and smaller improvements. Save it, label it, and move forward. You can refine it later in Chapter 8.
