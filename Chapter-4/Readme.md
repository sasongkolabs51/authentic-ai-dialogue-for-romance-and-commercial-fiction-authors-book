# Chapter 4: Build the Dialogue Matrix — The Six-Field Translation Workbook
*A practical workbook for applying the concepts from the chapter.*

## How to Use These Materials

This workbook converts the Dialogue Matrix construction method from Chapter 4 into a complete, ready-to-use building system. You will find the six-field framework, a layer-to-field translation method with worked examples, three prebuilt archetype templates you can adapt, a three-scene stress-test protocol with AI prompt templates, a refinement loop with iteration tracking, and a forty-five-minute sprint plan. Work through the sections in order during a single forty-five-minute session. You will need your completed Chapter 3 Seven-Layer Character Excavation profile, a blank document, your AI tool, and a timer. By the end, you will hold a stress-tested, version-labeled Dialogue Matrix ready to deploy in every future dialogue session.
