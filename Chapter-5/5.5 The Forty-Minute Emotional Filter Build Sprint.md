## 5.5 The Forty-Minute Emotional Filter Build Sprint

### 5.5.1 Filter Build Session Prep Checklist

- [ ] Chapter 3 Seven-Layer Character Excavation profiles open (both characters)
- [ ] Chapter 4 Dialogue Matrix documents open (both characters)
- [ ] Two blank documents ready (one per character's Emotional Filter)
- [ ] AI tool open (needed for Steps 3 and 4)
- [ ] Excavation-to-Filter Mapping Reference visible (section 5.2.1)
- [ ] Timer set for forty minutes

### 5.5.2 Forty-Minute Pacing Guide

| Time Block | Step | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–10:00 | Step 1 | Build the Three-Tier Emotional Filter for your grumpy hero |
| 10:00–20:00 | Step 2 | Build the Three-Tier Emotional Filter for your sunshine heroine |
| 20:00–30:00 | Step 3 | Generate a two-character scene using both filters |
| 30:00–38:00 | Step 4 | Verify emotional progression across the scene |
| 38:00–40:00 | Buffer | Read both filters aloud. Confirm the emotional logic activates. |

### 5.5.5 AI Prompt Template: Two-Character Scene with Emotional Filters

Use this template to assemble all your materials into a single generation instruction for Step 3 of the sprint.

---

**Copy the prompt below into your AI tool. Fill in every bracket before generating.**

> Generate a scene (six to eight lines of dialogue) between [CHARACTER_A_NAME] and [CHARACTER_B_NAME]. One character is trying to reach the other emotionally.
>
> [PASTE_CHARACTER_A_DIALOGUE_MATRIX]
>
> [PASTE_CHARACTER_A_THREE_TIER_EMOTIONAL_FILTER]
>
> [PASTE_CHARACTER_A_SUBTEXT_INJECTION_INSTRUCTION: "In this scene, [CHARACTER_A] is feeling [FELT_EMOTION] but expressing [EXPRESSED_EMOTION]..."]
>
> [PASTE_CHARACTER_B_DIALOGUE_MATRIX]
>
> [PASTE_CHARACTER_B_THREE_TIER_EMOTIONAL_FILTER]
>
> [PASTE_CHARACTER_B_SUBTEXT_INJECTION_INSTRUCTION: "In this scene, [CHARACTER_B] is feeling [FELT_EMOTION] but expressing [EXPRESSED_EMOTION]..."]
>
> Write the dialogue only. No narration. No stage directions. Let the subtext emerge from the rhythm, pacing, and word choice as specified in the filters.

**Result:**

- [ ] Both characters show a clear shift between the first half and the last two lines → Progression is working. The filter is complete.
- [ ] One character is static → Add a progression clause to that character's Trigger Response tier. Regenerate.
- [ ] Both characters are static → Add progression clauses to both Trigger Response tiers. Regenerate.

**If subtext is still inaudible after two generation passes:** Return to your Subtext Layer and add one more specific behavioral marker to the felt-emotion side. Replace abstract language such as “He feels scared” with an executable instruction like “The sentence arrives one beat late, and the character changes the subject before the other speaker can respond.” After you add that marker, regenerate the dialogue and test it again. The AI will then have concrete rhythmic and behavioral instructions to build the subtext from, and the gap you want will open.
