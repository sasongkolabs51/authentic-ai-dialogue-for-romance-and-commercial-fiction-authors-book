# Chapter 5: Design the Emotional Filter — The Three-Tier Subtext and Restraint Workbook
*A practical workbook for applying the concepts from the chapter.*

## How to Use These Materials

This workbook converts the Three-Tier Emotional Filter method from Chapter 5 into a complete, ready-to-use emotional encoding system. You will find three tier-building worksheets, two restraint-encoding templates, the Subtext Injection prompt technique, a decision tree for troubleshooting flat subtext, and a forty-minute sprint plan that produces two completed filters and one verified two-character scene. Work through sections 5.1 through 5.4 as reference and building material, then complete the sprint in section 5.5 during a single forty-minute session. You will need your Chapter 3 Seven-Layer Character Excavation profiles, your Chapter 4 Dialogue Matrices, two blank documents, your AI tool, and a timer. By the end, your characters' dialogue will carry the unspoken weight, tension, and guarded vulnerability that readers feel in their ribs.
