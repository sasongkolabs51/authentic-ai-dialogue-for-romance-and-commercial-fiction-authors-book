## 3.1 Common Pitfalls: Why Labels and Freeform Bios Fail

Before you begin the excavation, understand why your previous profiling attempts produced generic output. Each failure mode below points to the same root cause: surface descriptions leave the AI's default linguistic architecture untouched.

| Approach | What You Did | Why It Failed | What the AI Actually Received |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Freeform character bios** | Wrote paragraphs of backstory and personality description | The AI reads bios as background context, then generates using its default speech patterns | A vague mood; no structural instructions for sentence length, vocabulary, or rhythm |
| **Personality-type labels** | Typed "introvert," "sardonic," "sunshine" | Labels are categorical; the AI fills each category with its generic template | The same output it would produce for any character tagged with that label |
| **Vague behavioral notes** | Wrote "He speaks in short sentences. He has trust issues." | Descriptions float on top of the AI's defaults without restructuring them | A thin coat of paint over the same polite-verbose foundation |
