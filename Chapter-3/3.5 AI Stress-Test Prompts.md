## 3.5 AI Stress-Test Prompts

Run these three prompts after completing your profile. They reveal gaps, vagueness, and missed dimensions before you commit the profile to the Dialogue Matrix in Chapter 4. Allow ten minutes total for all three prompts.

### 3.5.1 AI Prompt Template: Emotional Specificity Test

**Purpose:** Checks whether your profile contains enough granular detail to produce emotionally precise dialogue.

---

**Copy the prompt below into your AI tool. Fill in the bracket before generating.**

> Based on the following psychological profile, generate three sample lines of dialogue this character would say when they are trying to hide that they are hurt.

| What one specific detail could I add to that layer to sharpen the output? | [YOUR_ADDITION] |

### 3.5.2 AI Prompt Template: Relational Range Test

**Purpose:** Checks whether your relational triggers and defense mechanisms produce distinct conversational behavior across relationship types.

---

**Copy the prompt below into your AI tool. Fill in the bracket before generating.**

> Based on the following psychological profile, write three short dialogue snippets (2–3 lines each) for this character:
>
> 1. Speaking to their love interest during a moment of vulnerability.
> 2. Speaking to their best friend during a casual, low-stakes conversation.
> 3. Speaking to their antagonist during a confrontation.
>
> Show how the character's speech shifts across these three contexts.
>
> [PASTE_YOUR_FULL_SEVEN_LAYER_PROFILE_HERE]

---

**Relational Differentiation Check:**

| Context | Did the voice shift? | What specifically changed? (cadence, vocabulary, register, rhythm) | Adjustment needed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Love interest | [Yes / No] | [DESCRIBE_THE_SHIFT] | [IF_NO_WHAT_TO_ADD] |
| Best friend | [Yes / No] | [DESCRIBE_THE_SHIFT] | [IF_NO_WHAT_TO_ADD] |
| Antagonist | [Yes / No] | [DESCRIBE_THE_SHIFT] | [IF_NO_WHAT_TO_ADD] |

If any row reads "No," return to Layer Four (Relational Triggers) or Layer Three (Defense Mechanisms) and add a conditional rule specific to that relationship type.

### 3.5.3 AI Prompt Template: Contradiction Crack Test

**Purpose:** Tests Layer Seven directly. Checks whether the AI can produce a convincing crack in the character's mask.

---

**Copy the prompt below into your AI tool. Fill in the bracket before generating.**

> Based on the following psychological profile, generate a short scene (4–6 lines of dialogue) where this character's aspirational self-image cracks under pressure. Show the contradiction emerging in their speech—the moment the mask fails and something raw slips through.
>
> [PASTE_YOUR_FULL_SEVEN_LAYER_PROFILE_HERE]

---

**Contradiction Quality Check:**

| Question | Your Assessment |
|---|---|
| Can I clearly hear the mask in the first half of the output? | [Yes / No] |
| Can I clearly hear the mask breaking in the second half? | [Yes / No] |
| Does the linguistic shift match what I documented in Layer Seven? | [Yes / No] |
| If the crack feels unconvincing, what specific detail is missing from my Layer Seven entry? | [YOUR_NOTE] |
