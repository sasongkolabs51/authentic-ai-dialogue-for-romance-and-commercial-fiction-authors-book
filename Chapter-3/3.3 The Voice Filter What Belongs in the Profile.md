## 3.3 The Voice Filter: What Belongs in the Profile

Apply this filter to every trait you identify during the excavation. It keeps your profile lean, voice-focused, and translatable into Dialogue Matrix parameters.

### 3.3.1 The Four-Gate Checklist

For each trait, ask:

- [ ] Does this trait change how the character **constructs a sentence**? (Length, structure, fragments vs. full thoughts)
- [ ] Does this trait change how the character **selects a word**? (Vocabulary ceiling, register, domain, formality)
- [ ] Does this trait change how the character **modulates their emotional register**? (Temperature, guardedness, leakage, subtext)
- [ ] Does this trait change how the character **sequences their conversational turns**? (Interrupting, trailing off, deflecting, monologuing)

**Result:**

- **At least one box checked →** This is a **voice trait**. Write it into your voice profile.
- **No boxes checked →** This is a **narration trait**. Move it to your character bible for action beats, body language, and scene description.

### 3.3.2 Practice Drill: Sort the Trait

Cover the answer column. Make your call. Then check.

| Trait | Your Call (Voice / Narration) | Answer and Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Callum cracks his knuckles before delivering bad news | [YOUR_CALL] | **Narration.** Physical action beat. Does not reshape sentences, vocabulary, or turn-taking. |
| Callum drops all contractions when delivering bad news, switching to full formal constructions | [YOUR_CALL] | **Voice.** The shift from "don't" to "do not" changes vocabulary register and sentence construction. |
| Mara stares at her shoes when receiving a compliment | [YOUR_CALL] | **Narration.** Body language. Paints the scene but does not alter the words she produces. |
| Mara deflects compliments by immediately asking three questions about the other person | [YOUR_CALL] | **Voice.** Reshapes conversational turns. She sequences speech differently under the trigger of praise. |
| Callum's jaw tightens before he forces out an apology | [YOUR_CALL] | **Narration.** Physical. Belongs in the action beat surrounding the dialogue. |
| Callum's apologies emerge as two-word fragments: "I'm sorry." No elaboration. | [YOUR_CALL] | **Voice.** Sentence construction clipped to two words. The brevity is the apology. |

**Quick rule when in doubt:** Could the AI reproduce this in dialogue alone, without any action beats or stage directions? If yes, it is voice. If it requires a body, a room, or a physical gesture to exist, it is narration.

### 3.3.3 Profile Quality Check

Run this sixty-second check before moving to the stress-test phase.

- [ ] Every trait in my profile passes at least one of the four voice-filter gates.
- [ ] No physical actions, body language, or environmental observations remain in the voice profile.
- [ ] Each layer contains between five and eight entries. (Fewer than five: dig deeper. More than eight: trim to the strongest.)
- [ ] I can read each trait aloud and immediately hear the character's voice shift.
- [ ] The profile exists as a typed or handwritten document, saved and labeled, ready for Chapter 4.
